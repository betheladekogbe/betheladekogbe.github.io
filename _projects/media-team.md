## The challenge

Create a sustainable way for new volunteers to learn technical production roles and gain confidence in real services.

## The approach

A four-service progression guided people from observation to independent execution: watch, assist, perform with assistance, and perform with observation. Workshops and peer mentorship supported the learning process.

## My contribution

I helped lead the media team, supported technical training, coordinated volunteer development, and built opportunities for team members to take ownership.

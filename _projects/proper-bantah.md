*Proper Bantah* is a talk show created by Rasheed Peters in 2018 to give people a place to tell their stories while offering entertaining, accessible inspiration. It premiered at Brandeis University on January 18, 2019.

As **Technical Director**, I worked across filming and post-production. I operated cameras, recorded interview audio, and helped set up and break down each shoot. I also cleaned up episode audio and **created all of the show's original music**, including its intro and background tracks.

I edited alongside the team on the full interviews, often putting together first cuts so other editors could focus on clean-up and final revisions. With most of us balancing classes and other jobs, that teamwork was essential to meeting our weekly deadlines.

I had full creative control over the promotional videos: choosing interesting interview moments, pulling funny clips from the *in the field* segments, and making horizontal and vertical edits with graphics and captions. One of my favorites was the Jonathan Goldman interview. We played indoor racquetball for his field segment, and I turned the footage into an **ESPN-style highlight reel** for the episode intro and promotion.

Our videos encouraged people to watch the show and attend in-person tapings. Across Instagram, Facebook and YouTube, the show reached **more than 7,500 combined views** (reported).

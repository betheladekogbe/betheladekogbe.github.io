On **April 20, 2025**, Pentecostal Tabernacle held three Easter Sunday services at its South campus in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Each service required live production, timed speaker and worship transitions, photography, audio, on-screen graphics, and reliable coordination between technical and platform teams.

I prepared the individual call sheets and run-of-show documents and served as **producer and technical director**, including switching the live program feed for the later services. The work called for one consistent production standard across three consecutive services, while accommodating different staffing, stage elements, and speakers.

Each run of show documented role coverage, individual call times, audio checks, rehearsal and huddle windows, graphics cues, stage transitions, and planned livestream elements. The documents specified which crew members would operate cameras, switching, graphics, front-of-house and livestream audio, and photography.

My challenge was to balance operational precision with the practical reality of running several events in sequence. The second and third productions began with much shorter setup windows than the first.

## A production built around cues

The original 9:00 AM plan began early with equipment setup, separate soundcheck and media-check windows, a pre-service huddle, and countdown content. The later run-of-show versions covered the distinct camera positions and graphics staff available at 10:30 AM and noon.

These weren't just schedules. They were shared operational documents connecting the live visual output with the audio, stage, worship, and speaking teams. They made it possible to assign tasks in advance, explain what came next, and respond when a service moved faster or slower than expected.

## What this demonstrates

- **Technical direction:** Operated and coordinated the live video program, including switching, graphics, and media playback.
- **Production management:** Prepared three separate run-of-show documents rather than using a generic event template.
- **Team coordination:** Built coverage plans for 22 assignments across six, seven, and nine positions per service.
- **Communication:** Distributed call times and department cues to help audio, worship, media, and stage teams work in sync.

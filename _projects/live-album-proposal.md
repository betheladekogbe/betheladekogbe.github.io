A major live music event can have a life beyond the night itself. After Pentecostal Tabernacle's **2024 Worship Night**, I proposed developing a **live album** from the performances captured during the event.

In November 2024, I wrote and shared a proposal titled **In His Presence**, asking PT's creative leadership to consider releasing the songs as a recorded project.

## The strategic idea

The opportunity was to turn an event-based production into a lasting audio product: a set of recordings the community could revisit and share beyond the livestream.

Moving from a live recording to an album also raised practical questions. The concept needed to be evaluated with the people responsible for worship, production, mixing, and distribution before it could become a release.

## What the record confirms

- **November 22, 2024:** I distributed the formal album proposal to PT leadership.
- **November 22–27:** The concept received encouraging feedback and was referred for further discussion.
- **January 14, 2025:** An assigned follow-up task referenced obtaining mixing numbers for the **WN 24 Live Album**.

These records establish the idea, my authorship of the proposal, leadership interest, and an initial follow-up step. They **do not establish that the album was completed or commercially released**, so this case study is presented as a strategic concept rather than a published album.

## Why it belongs in my portfolio

This work reflects my interest in creative leadership beyond executing assigned tasks. I looked at an event we had already produced, identified an opportunity for additional storytelling and distribution, prepared a proposal, and initiated discussion about what production would require.

It is an example of **taking initiative, articulating a creative opportunity, and connecting media production to a longer-term content strategy**.

## The purpose

Create an accessible video series that explains the biblical significance of each day of Holy Week, connects those stories to Jesus, and invites viewers to participate in Easter services.

## The audience

The series was created for both existing church members and people outside the church, including audiences with different levels of familiarity with Holy Week.

## The approach

We paired biblical and historical context with simple, impactful language and purposeful visuals, aiming to make each episode concise and meaningful.

## My contribution

I contributed to creative direction, production coordination, and post-production across the series. The project brought together educational storytelling and outreach-focused media production.

In the spring of 2026, Pentecostal Tabernacle prepared to recognize Bishop Brian Greene's 31st pastoral anniversary. One media component was a recap film drawing on footage from the previous year's anniversary gala.

My role was **developing the creative brief, assigning and supervising the edit, and coordinating the production handoff**. The editor was responsible for the actual assembly; my job was to communicate the story we wanted to tell and set the editorial direction.

## Framing the story

This wasn't intended to be a chronological record of every part of the gala. The brief called for a short video that felt **high-energy, tasteful, celebratory, and personally focused on the honoree**.

The archival source material also included people presenting awards and offering spoken tributes. I emphasized those recordings as a key part of the story, so that the finished piece would have emotional substance rather than functioning as a silent montage.

## What I brought to the project

This project demonstrates a part of production leadership that's easy to overlook: writing a brief that gives an editor enough creative freedom while establishing clear boundaries for the outcome.

I defined **what the viewer should feel**, which source material mattered most, where the piece would be shown, and how reviews would fit into the timeline. I then supported the transition from an editorial project into a live-event playback asset.

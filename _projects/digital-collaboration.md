In June 2026, a guest speaker from Aletheia Church joined a Pentecostal Tabernacle Sunday service. The event created an opportunity for both organizations to extend the reach of the recorded message.

As PT's Production Lead, I worked with the social-media team to coordinate the content handoff and contact Aletheia's communications partners about cross-platform publication.

## Connecting production to distribution

One event generated several different kinds of material: the full sermon recording, event photography, and material for a short social recap.

In my June 9 outreach, I introduced our team, connected PT's social-media manager with an external partner contact, and requested coordination with the people managing Aletheia's YouTube and Instagram presence.

The communication continued the following day with a YouTube collaboration invitation and the link to the relevant video.

## What this demonstrates

My primary contribution here was coordination, not ownership of all the photographs, video editing, or publishing decisions. I helped connect people, route assets, and make collaboration easier for the teams involved.

That kind of work matters in production leadership because **distribution depends on relationships and workflows as much as it depends on the final media file**.

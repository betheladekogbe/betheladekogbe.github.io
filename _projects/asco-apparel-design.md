For **ASCOU at Allison-Smith Company**, I completed two separate racing-inspired shirt graphic commissions in summer 2026: an **ASCO 101** design and an **ASCOU Pit Stop** design.

My work focused on **graphic composition, adapting and manipulating existing visual assets, typography, layout, branding, and client revisions**. I did not draw the car or other illustration elements from scratch; I selected, combined, and modified graphics into cohesive branded designs.

## Commission 01 · ASCO 101 Racing Graphic

The first assignment called for a NASCAR-inspired front-and-back graphic featuring **"ASCO 101," "Learn. Apply. Lead.," a No. 43 race car**, and ASCOU branding. I submitted multiple concepts and mockups, then adapted the car imagery and composition after feedback. The client selected a preferred variation, and I delivered the transparent final artwork in August 2026.

## Commission 02 · ASCOU Pit Stop

For the second commission I developed **three initial concepts** around a pit-stop/racetrack theme. After the client selected a direction, I revised the figures, track details, race-car elements, typography, and layout in several feedback rounds. I also prepared a separate **"ASCOU PIT STOP"** back-of-shirt graphic. Final digital artwork was delivered on **September 2, 2026**.

## Process and Outcome

Across both commissions, I combined and modified existing visual assets, applied brand elements, developed variations, and responded to detailed client revisions. The result was **two client-approved digital design commissions**, with final artwork delivered.

The work is presented as **digital apparel artwork**. Whether physical shirts were manufactured is not confirmed.
